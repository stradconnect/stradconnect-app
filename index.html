Construction document coordination

Access files anytime, anywhere.Your drawings. Your revisions. In your hands.

Organize your drawings, control every revision, and keep every request moving with STRAD CONNECT. Designed specifically for the construction industry, STRAD CONNECT provides a secure, centralized workspace where architects, structural consultants, MEP engineers, contractors, PMCs, and other project stakeholders can store, share, and manage construction documents efficiently.

Every drawing revision is automatically tracked, giving teams access to the latest version while preserving a complete history of previous revisions. When a required drawing is missing or pending, teams can raise a request directly to the responsible discipline, while the receiving discipline can track, prioritize, and attend those requests without missing critical deadlines.

With role-based access, structured project folders, revision control, and request-based coordination, STRAD CONNECT reduces confusion, minimizes coordination delays, and keeps the entire project team connected through one controlled source of truth.
